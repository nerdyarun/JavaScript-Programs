# Parental, Maternity & Paternity Leave Policy

## 1. Maternity Leave
- Birth mothers are entitled to **16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave**.
- Maternity leave can begin up to 4 weeks prior to the expected delivery date.
- Additional unpaid maternity leave up to 8 weeks may be granted upon request, subject to executive approval.

## 2. Paternity & Secondary Caregiver Leave
- Fathers and secondary caregivers are entitled to **4 weeks of fully paid parental leave**.
- This leave must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption placement.

## 3. Adoption & Surrogacy Leave
- Primary adoptive parents receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Secondary adoptive parents receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave.

## 4. Notice Period
- Employees must notify HR and their direct manager at least **30 days prior** to the intended leave start date, providing expected dates and medical documentation.
