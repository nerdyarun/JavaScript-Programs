# Annual Leave & Vacation Policy

## 1. Entitlement & Accrual
- All full-time employees accrue paid annual leave at a rate of 1.66 days per completed calendar month of service, totaling **20 days of paid annual leave per year**.
- Part-time employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rata basis.
- Probationary employees are eligible to take accrued leave after completing 3 months of employment.

## 2. Carry-Over & Rollover Rules
- Employees are encouraged to take their annual leave within the calendar year accrued.
- A maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** can be rolled over into the following calendar year.
- Any additional unused days beyond 5 days will be forfeited at the end of the year on December 31st unless written approval is granted by HR and Executive Management.

## 3. Request & Approval Workflow
- Leave requests of 3 days or fewer must be submitted via the HR portal at least 3 business days in advance.
- Leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days require at least 14 days advance notice.
- Manager approval is required for all annual leave requests.
