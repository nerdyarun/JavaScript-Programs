# Casual, Bereavement & Unpaid Personal Leave

## 1. Casual Leave
- Employees receive **5 days of paid casual leave** per year for urgent personal matters, administrative work, or unforeseen short emergencies.
- Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave and cannot exceed 2 consecutive days per request.

## 2. Bereavement Leave
- Employees are granted up to **5 days of fully paid leave** in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- Up to 3 days of paid leave is provided for extended family members (grandparent, in-law).

## 3. Unpaid Leave
- Unpaid leave may be requested when all accrued paid leave options are exhausted.
- Approvals for unpaid leave are evaluated case-by-case and require approval from both the Department Head and HR Director.
