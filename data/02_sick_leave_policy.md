# Sick Leave & Medical Leave Policy

## 1. Sick Leave Allowance
- Full-time employees receive **10 paid sick leave days per calendar year**, available from their first day of employment.
- Sick leave is intended for personal illness, medical appointments, injury, or caring for an immediate family member who is ill.

## 2. Documentation & Verification Requirements
- A formal **medical certificate from a licensed physician** is mandatory for any sick leave absence exceeding **3 consecutive business days**.
- HR reserves the right to request a doctor's note for frequent short-term sick absences.

## 3. Rollover & Encashment
- Unused sick leave does **not roll over** into the subsequent calendar year.
- Unused sick leave cannot be encashed upon resignation or termination.
